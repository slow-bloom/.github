# Hi there, we are Slow Bloom 🌱

> **Move with intention, bloom at your own pace.**

Slow Bloom is an indie studio dedicated to building thoughtful, minimalist tools for runners, yogis, and movement enthusiasts. We believe endurance and wellness are not about rushing the outcome, but nurturing the practice over time.

---

### 🏃‍♂️ What We Build

We explore the intersection of technology, endurance sports, and mindfulness:

- **Runner Utilities & Calculators**: Open-source tools for pace calculations, heart rate zones, race pacing strategies, and training metrics.
- **Mindful Movement Tools**: Utilities supporting intentional routines across running, yoga, and physical recovery.
- **Web & Mobile Experiments**: Lightweight web apps, scripts, and open libraries for fitness tracking and data processing.
